Our story

How KHRC began.

The idea of Kanzul Hikmah began with a dream — to establish an Islamic research centre that could unify brilliant minds and offer them a field in which their intelligence and ability could serve Islam. On 17 December 2019, that vision was shared with a group of University of Dhaka students, who joined the task. On 16 March 2020, Kanzul Hikmah Research Centre was formally inaugurated by Shaykh Saqib Iqbal Shami. Today it brings together researchers, professionals, and students from many universities and institutions around a single purpose: knowledge, in service of peace.

Our vision

In today's world, Islam is too often mischaracterised — an anxiety we know as Islamophobia — while many come to see faith itself as an obstacle to a happy life. Accepting that challenge, we work to present Islam as a religion of peace and to mitigate Islamophobia across the world. We strive to unify the Ummah around the authentic message of the Holy Qur'an and the guidance of the Prophet ﷺ, to advance knowledge, and to build an intellectual society — enlightening a darkening world with the light of the Qur'an.

What we believe

Kanzul Hikmah is committed to the Aqīdah of Ahl-e-Sunnat wal Jamā'at, and works — free of political affiliation — to convey it with clarity, adab, and respect across the Muslim community.

From the Director General

I would first like to thank our chief guest, Shaykh Sahibzada Sayed Saifuddin Ahmad al-Hasani wal-Husaini al-Maijbhandari.
