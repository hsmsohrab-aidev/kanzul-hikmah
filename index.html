Brief introduction

Who we are

Kanzul Hikmah Research Centre (KHRC) is a non-political platform responsible for knowledge creation and dissemination in Islamic Education, Social, Economics and Finance, and for fostering Islamic awareness to contribute to sustainable development in different arenas of the world.

KHRC works for people with a motto of positive impression in peace and happiness. We believe in human rights, decent living conditions, and human dignity. Through innovative research we highlight peaceful historical narratives and promote a positive view of Islam worldwide.

The centre is formed by researchers, Islamic scholars, and students from different universities and institutions.

Our vision

Promoting peace through knowledge Mitigating Islamophobia · Unifying Ummah

In today’s world, Islam is often misrepresented through Islamophobia, while many see religion as an obstacle to a happy life. Accepting this challenge, we work to promote Islam as a peaceful way of life and to mitigate Islamophobia worldwide.

Correct message

Spreading the correct message of the Holy Quran and the advice of the Prophet ﷺ among Muslims.

Unity

Working to unify the Muslim Ummah through shared knowledge and understanding.

Research space

Offering a dedicated research environment for scholars and young researchers.

Intellectual society

Establishing an intellectual society enlightened by the light of the Holy Quran.
