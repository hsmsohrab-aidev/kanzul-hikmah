About the centre

Who we are

Kanzul Hikmah Research Centre (KHRC) is a non-political platform responsible for knowledge creation and dissemination in Islamic Education, Social, Economics and Finance, and for fostering Islamic awareness that contributes to sustainable development across the world.

KHRC works with a motto of positive impression in peace and happiness. We believe in human rights, decent living conditions and human dignity. Through innovative research we highlight peaceful historical narratives and promote a positive view of Islam worldwide.

The centre is formed by researchers, Islamic scholars and students from different universities and institutions.

Vision

Promoting peace through knowledge

We work to present Islam as a peaceful way of life, mitigate Islamophobia, unify the Muslim Ummah and build an intellectual society guided by the light of the Holy Quran.

Correct message

Spreading the correct message of the Holy Quran and the advice of the Prophet ﷺ among Muslims.

Unity

Working to unify the Muslim Ummah through shared knowledge and understanding.

Research space

Offering a dedicated research environment for scholars and young researchers.

Intellectual society

Establishing an intellectual society enlightened by the light of the Holy Quran.

Activities

What KHRC does

Six pillars that shape our research, education and community work.

  1. 01

    Research & Publication

    Books, translations, series and scholarly publications for knowledge dissemination.

  2. 02

    Monthly Dawah Programme

    Regular programmes for awareness, learning and community engagement.

  3. 03

    Media Activities

    Graphics, video and social media to share research and positive messaging.

  4. 04

    Annual Summit

    Yearly gathering for scholars, students and well-wishers to exchange ideas.

  5. 05

    Social & Humanitarian

    Community service and humanitarian initiatives grounded in compassion.

  6. 06

    Academic Programme

    Day-long, monthly and half-yearly courses to spread structured knowledge.
