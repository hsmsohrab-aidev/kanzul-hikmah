Who we are

Kanzul Hikmah is a platform for knowledge creation and dissemination in Islamic education, society, economics, and finance — fostering awareness that contributes to sustainable, peaceful development. Founded by a circle of researchers and university students, we work to spread the light of authentic scholarship, mitigate Islamophobia, and present Islam, in every arena, as a religion of peace.
