Al-Mustafa Institute

Authentic knowledge, traditionally transmitted.

The academic wing of Kanzul Hikmah — offering authentic instruction in the Islamic sciences, social sciences, and liberal arts.

About the Institute

Al-Mustafa Institute is the academic concern of Kanzul Hikmah, established in 2020 to provide authentic knowledge in the Islamic sciences, as well as the social sciences and liberal arts. In a time when scholarship is too readily claimed and too easily counterfeited — leaving people misguided and society unsettled — Al-Mustafa exists to transmit knowledge through qualified scholars grounded in traditional scholarship.
