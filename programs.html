Let's Know — Talk show on contemporary issues

Season 1 · 4 episodes

1. COVID-19: How Far Is the Vaccine?

With Dr. Sharif Mohiuddin (Research Fellow, Aichi Medical University, Japan) on hygiene and reassurance during the pandemic. Hosted by DG Sultan Mahmud Sohal.

2. Which Is Important: A Labourer's Life or RMG?

With Adv. Muhammad Iqbal Hasan (Chairman, Law Dept., UITS), Abdul Alim (President & CEO, Sustainable Management Systems Inc.), and Amirul Islam (Chairman, Lupdot Fashion Ltd.).

3. The Significant Night of Qadr

With Shaykh Ihsan Iqbal Qadri (Sri Lanka), Dr. Mufti Baktiar Uddin (Jamia Ahmadia Sunnia Alia), and Dr. Muhammad Nasir Uddin. Hosted by Hasan Mohammad Sharfuddin.

4. Eid in Pandemic Days

With Shaykh Saiful Azam al-Azhari (Al-Nur Islamic School, UK), Adv. Muhammad Iqbal Hasan, and Chowdhury Mohammad Mahbubul Alam. Hosted by DG Sultan Mahmud Sohal.
